Your on-call engineer spends the first 20 minutes of every incident doing the same thing: grep logs, check dashboards, scan deploys, form a hypothesis.

That's not engineering. That's context assembly.

At Saarthi, we build AI assistants for doctors in ERs and ICUs — same core challenge as incident response. Get the right context to the decision-maker, fast. Context engineering. Same discipline, different domains.

The industry is already moving here — Datadog's Bits AI SRE, incident.io, Rootly. But the real question is: what should an engineer actually spend their time on during an incident? Collecting logs isn't it. Those require attention, not judgment.
