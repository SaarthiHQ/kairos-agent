An LLM is a next-token predictor. An expert has calibrated self-knowledge, domain compression, judgment under ambiguity, principled refusal, and persistent knowledge. Six approaches are being tried to close the gap. None of them alone does it.

The Six Approaches

1. Fine-Tuning — Change How the Model Behaves

Industry standard
LoRA, QLoRA, DPO, RLHF. Adjusts weights on domain data for tone, format, safety patterns.
Works for: behavior consistency, domain vocabulary, output format
Doesn't solve: hallucination (reduces frequency, not eliminated), epistemic awareness, genuine refusal

2026 consensus: fine-tune for behavior, not knowledge. Don't rely on it for factual accuracy.

2. RAG — Change What the Model Sees

Industry standard
Vector search, BM25, hybrid retrieval, GraphRAG, re-ranking. Injects documents at inference time.
Works for: current knowledge, citable sources, reduced hallucination (when answer is in context)
Doesn't solve: retrieval quality ceiling, no domain scoring, no compression, no quality assessment
Context engineering is what RAG becomes when you take it seriously. RAG gives you documents. CE gives you the right 50 lines, scored and structured for the specific question.

3. Context Engineering — Systematic Input Engineering

Saarthi (built)
Multi-source fetch, domain scoring, compression, token budgets, prompt repetition, quality assessment.
Works for: domain compression, partial self-knowledge, partial refusal, model tier arbitrage (24x cost reduction)
Doesn't solve: refusal is behavioral not structural, ambiguity judgment is prompt-dependent, no persistence
85-90% of expert behavior on structured tasks. The engineering approximation of expertise while architecture catches up.

4. Uncertainty Quantification — Measure What the Model Knows

Active research
Semantic entropy (Nature 2024), evidential deep learning (Dirichlet outputs), behavioral calibration (Rewarding Doubt, Dec 2025).
Works for: detecting when model is uncertain, calibrating expressed confidence with actual accuracy
Doesn't solve: high-confidence hallucinations (model is wrong AND certain), 5-10x compute cost for semantic entropy
Rewarding Doubt (Dec 2025): Smaller models trained with calibration-aware RL surpass frontier models on UQ. Calibration is a transferable meta-skill, decoupled from raw accuracy. Validates our thesis.

5. Abstention Training — Teach the Model to Refuse

Unsolved
AbstentionBench (Jun 2025), Know Your Limits survey (TACL 2025). Training models to say "I don't know."
Works for: simple cases (obviously unanswerable questions)
Doesn't solve: reliable abstention at scale — no model reliably abstains across diverse question types

AbstentionBench: Scaling doesn't help

Larger models are NOT better at abstaining. And reasoning fine-tuning HURTS abstention — DeepSeek R1 shows a 24% drop in abstention vs its non-reasoning counterpart. Teaching models to think harder makes them LESS likely to say "I don't know."

Implication for Saarthi

If training the model to abstain doesn't work, and making it smarter makes it worse, then external constraint enforcement is not just a shortcut — it may be the ONLY reliable approach with current architectures. This validates our constraint layer strategy and the SLI hypothesis.

6. Neuro-Symbolic AI — Add Structure to the Model

Emerging (2026)
Neural perception + symbolic reasoning. Knowledge graphs, formal verification (TLA+, deontic logic), explicit constraint engines.
Works for: constraint enforcement by construction, explainable reasoning, domain invariant preservation
Doesn't solve: brittle neural-symbolic interfaces, scaling cost, neural component still hallucinates (symbolic catches it post-hoc)
SLI goes further: Instead of running symbolic reasoning alongside the neural model, embed constraints INTO the latent space. Avoids brittle interfaces, maintains differentiability. h = [h_text, h_domain, h_constraints].

The Four Stages to Expert

1

External Intelligence

Now (built)

Context engineering + structural constraints. 85-95% of expert behavior. Works with any model.

2

Calibrated Intelligence

6-12 months

Behavioral calibration (Rewarding Doubt) + persistent memory. Confidence correlates with accuracy.

3

Constrained Intelligence

12-24 months

Constraint projection layer. Domain invariants in the forward pass. Invalid outputs probabilistically suppressed.

4

Architecturally Expert

2+ years

Factorized latent: h = [h_text, h_domain, h_constraints]. Invalid outputs structurally unreachable. Native "I don't know."

What No One Else Is Doing

1. No unified framework across context engineering + constraints + domain specificity

LangChain does orchestration. LlamaIndex does retrieval. Mem0 does memory. Nobody does domain-aware scoring + compression + quality assessment + constraint enforcement as a single pipeline.

2. No cross-domain extraction of common patterns

Everyone is vertical. We apply the same framework to healthcare AND incident management, extracting the horizontal discipline.

3. No latent-space constraint embedding for generative models

Evidential deep learning does it for classification. Neuro-symbolic uses external components. SLI proposes native, end-to-end differentiable constraint embedding.

4. AbstentionBench validates our architectural thesis

Reasoning models are WORSE at abstention. If making models smarter makes them worse at knowing limits, the solution must be structural. That's SLI.
